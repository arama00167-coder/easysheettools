Yes. Every template is built with standard formulas that work identically in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. Download the file, upload it to Google Drive, and open it with Sheets.

All of them. Template amounts are plain numbers with no fixed currency symbol, and the calculators include a currency selector (USD, GBP, EUR, PKR, INR, AED, CAD, AUD).
