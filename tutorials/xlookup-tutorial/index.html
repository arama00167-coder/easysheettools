How to Use XLOOKUP in Excel

XLOOKUP is the modern replacement for VLOOKUP — simpler, safer and able to look left. This guide walks you through it with a real product-price example you can copy.

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Reading time: about 5 minutes · Includes a free practice file

What is XLOOKUP, in plain words?

XLOOKUP finds something in one column and returns a value from another column in the same row. Type a product name, get its price. Type an employee ID, get the department. It is available in Excel 365, Excel 2021 and newer, and in Google Sheets.

The XLOOKUP formula

=XLOOKUP(what to find, where to look, what to return, [if not found])

Only three parts are required. The optional 4th part replaces the ugly #N/A error with your own message, such as "Not found".

Step-by-step example: find a product price

Imagine a price list — products in column A, prices in column B:

Product (A)Price (B)
USB Cable8.50
Wireless Mouse15.99
Keyboard29.50
  1. Click the cell where you want the price to appear.
  2. Type =XLOOKUP("Wireless Mouse", A2:A4, B2:B4, "Not found")
  3. Press Enter — the answer is 15.99.
  4. Better still: put the product name in a cell (say D2) and use =XLOOKUP(D2, A2:A4, B2:B4, "Not found") — now you can look up anything by typing it in D2.

XLOOKUP vs VLOOKUP — why XLOOKUP wins

XLOOKUPVLOOKUP
Can look to the LEFTYesNo
Default matchExact (safe)Approximate (dangerous!)
"Not found" messageBuilt inNeeds IFERROR
Breaks if you insert a columnNoYes, often

Learn VLOOKUP too (next tutorial) because older files are full of it — but write new formulas with XLOOKUP.

Three tips from real use

  • Lock your ranges with $ when copying a formula down: A$2:A$100.
  • Search from the bottom for the latest entry: add , , , -1 at the end to search last-to-first.
  • Return several columns at once — make the return range two columns wide and XLOOKUP spills both answers.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I get #NAME? for XLOOKUP?

Your Excel version is too old — XLOOKUP needs Excel 365 or Excel 2021+. In older versions, use VLOOKUP or INDEX+MATCH instead.

Does XLOOKUP work in Google Sheets?

Yes, XLOOKUP was added to Google Sheets and works with the same basic syntax.

What is the difference between exact and approximate match?

XLOOKUP uses exact match by default — it finds the precise value or reports not found. That is the safe behaviour you want for names, IDs and products.
