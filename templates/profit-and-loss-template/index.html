How to use it — step by step

  1. Choose your period (month, quarter or year) and enter it at the top.
  2. Add your revenue lines — sales, services and other income.
  3. Enter what the sold goods cost you (COGS) to get gross profit.
  4. List your operating expenses one by one.
  5. Read your net profit — and see exactly where the money went.

The formula, explained simply

Net Profit = Gross Profit − Total Expenses

Gross Profit = Total Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold. Each section uses SUM, so the statement is always internally consistent — the same structure used in professional accounting.

A worked example

Revenue of 25,000 minus COGS of 11,000 gives a gross profit of 14,000. After 8,300 of expenses, net profit is 5,700 — the worked example inside the file.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this P&L for a loan application?

Yes, this is the standard format banks ask for. Fill it with your real figures and print or save as PDF.

What is COGS exactly?

Cost of goods sold — what you paid for the products you sold (materials, purchase price, direct labour). Rent and salaries go in expenses instead.
