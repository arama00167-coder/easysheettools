What's inside this inventory management template

  • Item list with SKU, quantity, unit cost and selling price
  • Automatic stock value (quantity × cost) and total inventory value
  • Reorder level per item with red LOW STOCK alerts
  • Status column updates itself — no manual checking
  • Great for shops, warehouses and online sellers

How to use it — step by step

  1. List every product with its SKU, opening quantity and unit cost.
  2. Set a reorder level for each item (the point where you re-order).
  3. Update quantities when stock arrives or sells.
  4. Watch the Status column — items turn to LOW STOCK in red automatically.
  5. Check the total stock value at the top for your accounts.

The formula, explained simply

Status = IF(Quantity <= Reorder Level, "LOW STOCK", "OK")

Conditional formatting paints low-stock rows red. Stock Value = Quantity × Unit Cost, and the header total sums the value of everything you hold.
