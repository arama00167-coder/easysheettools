How to use it — step by step

  1. Write your fixed classes into the weekly grid first.
  2. Add study sessions for each subject around them.
  3. Pick subjects from the dropdown so colours stay consistent.
  4. Each morning, list today's topics in the checklist.
  5. On Sunday, check the hours summary — did every subject get time?

The formula, explained simply

Hours per Subject = COUNTIF(week grid, subject) × slot length

Counting your planned sessions per subject exposes imbalance early — if Maths has 6 slots and English has 1, next week's plan fixes itself.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours should I study per day?

Quality beats quantity: 3–5 focused hours with breaks beats 10 distracted ones. The planner helps you protect those hours.

Can I plan for exam weeks differently?

Yes — save a copy of the file for exam week and fill it with revision sessions only.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in the Excel or Google Sheets app — tick off topics from the checklist anywhere.
