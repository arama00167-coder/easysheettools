What's inside this gpa calculator template

  • Up to 10 subjects per semester — add more rows anytime
  • Grade dropdown (A, A−, B+, … F) with automatic grade points
  • Credit-weighted GPA on the standard 4.0 scale
  • Separate Grade Scale sheet explaining every letter
  • Total credits and quality points shown for your records

How to use it — step by step

  1. List your subjects in the first column.
  2. Enter each subject's credit hours.
  3. Pick your letter grade from the dropdown.
  4. Read your GPA in the highlighted box at the bottom.
  5. Save a copy each semester to track your progress.

The formula, explained simply

GPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credits) ÷ Total Credits

Grade points are found with VLOOKUP from the Grade Scale sheet — the same lookup skill taught in our tutorials. Weighted by credits, a 4-credit A lifts your GPA more than a 2-credit A.

A worked example

Five subjects — grades A, B+, A−, B and A across 17 credits — produce a GPA of 3.55 in the sample semester inside the file.

Frequently asked questions

Which GPA scale does this use?

The standard US 4.0 scale: A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, and so on down to F = 0.0. The full scale is printed on the Grade Scale sheet.

Can I calculate my CGPA (all semesters)?

Yes — add every subject from every semester into one long list, or average your semester GPAs weighted by each semester's credits.
