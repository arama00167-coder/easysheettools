What's inside this monthly budget template

  • Income section for salary, freelance and other earnings
  • Planned vs Actual columns with automatic difference
  • Expense groups: housing, food, transport, bills, fun and more
  • Savings line treated like a bill — pay yourself first
  • Summary box: total income, total spent and amount left

How to use it — step by step

  1. Enter this month's income at the top.
  2. Write your planned amounts for each expense group.
  3. During the month, fill the Actual column with what you really spent.
  4. Watch the Difference column — negative red numbers show overspending.
  5. Move what's left into savings using our Savings Goal Tracker.

The formula, explained simply

Difference = Planned − Actual

Section totals use SUM and the summary subtracts total spending from total income. Conditional formatting turns negative differences red so problem areas stand out instantly.

A worked example

On an income of 3,000, the sample budget plans 2,650 of spending and 350 of savings — and the actual column shows a 60 overspend on dining out to learn from next month.

Frequently asked questions

Does this budget template work in Google Sheets?

Yes — it was built for both. Upload it to Drive and it also becomes your 'monthly budget template Google Sheets' — same file, same formulas.

What budget rule should I follow?

A popular start is 50/30/20: 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings. Adjust the planned column to match your life.

Can couples or families share it?

Yes. In Google Sheets you can share the file so both partners update the same budget.
