What's inside this freelancer income tracker

  • Columns for client, platform, gross amount, fee % and net received
  • Upwork and Fiverr fee presets (20% / 10% / 5% tiers)
  • Monthly totals so tax time stops being scary
  • Per-client totals — see who your best clients really are
  • Works for direct clients too (0% fee)

How to use it — step by step

  1. Add one row for every payment you receive.
  2. Pick the platform and check the fee % is right.
  3. Enter the gross amount the client paid.
  4. The fee and your net received calculate automatically.
  5. Review the monthly summary before you withdraw or spend.

The formula, explained simply

Net Received = Gross − (Gross × Fee %)

Upwork charges 20% on the first $500 billed to a client, 10% up to $10,000 and 5% beyond that; Fiverr takes a flat 20%. The tracker applies the right percentage per row.

A worked example

Four sample payments — two Upwork, one Fiverr, one direct — gross 2,150 and net 1,905 after 245 of platform fees. That gap is why this tracker exists.

Frequently asked questions

How do Upwork fees work exactly?

Per client: 20% on lifetime billings up to $500, then 10% until $10,000, then 5%. Our free Upwork Fee Calculator shows the maths for any single job.

And Fiverr fees?

Fiverr takes 20% of every order including tips. Our Fiverr Fee Calculator helps you price gigs so you still earn your target.
