What's inside this marks sheet template

  • 6 subjects with obtained marks per student
  • Automatic total, percentage and letter grade
  • Class position (rank) calculated for every student
  • Top scorer highlighted — no manual sorting
  • Ready to print as the official class result sheet

How to use it — step by step

  1. Write the class, exam name and each subject's total marks.
  2. Enter student names and roll numbers.
  3. Type the obtained marks subject by subject.
  4. Totals, percentages, grades and positions appear instantly.
  5. Print the sheet or save it as PDF for records.

The formula, explained simply

Position = RANK(student total, all totals)

Percentage = Total Obtained ÷ Total Marks × 100, the grade comes from a lookup table (90+ = A+, 80+ = A…), and RANK orders every student automatically.

In the sample class of 10 students, the topper scores 542 out of 600 (90.3%, grade A+) — and the sheet finds and highlights them for you.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use fewer or more subjects?

Yes. The sample uses 6 subjects; delete a subject column or copy one to add more — totals adjust automatically.

Can students use it for their own marks?

Of course — enter just your own row to get your percentage and grade, or use our online Test Grade Calculator for a single test.

Does it show who failed a subject?

Low marks are easy to spot, and you can add a minimum-marks rule with conditional formatting — the How to Use sheet explains how.
