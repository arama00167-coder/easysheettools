What's inside this savings goal tracker

  • Goal name, target amount and target date at the top
  • Deposit log with dates and running total saved
  • Progress % and amount-remaining calculated automatically
  • Monthly deposits summary to build the habit
  • One sheet per goal — copy it for every new dream

How to use it — step by step

  1. Write your goal and the target amount (for example: Laptop — 1,200).
  2. Log your first deposit with today's date.
  3. Add a row every time you save, even small amounts.
  4. Check your progress % — it updates with every deposit.
  5. Celebrate at 100%, then copy the sheet for your next goal.

The formula, explained simply

Progress % = Total Saved ÷ Goal Amount

The running total uses a cumulative SUM down the deposit log, and the remaining amount is simply Goal − Saved, so you always know the exact gap.

A worked example

For a 1,200 laptop goal, deposits of 200, 150 and 250 build a total of 600 — exactly 50% of the goal, shown in the progress box.

Frequently asked questions

Can I track several goals in one file?

Yes — duplicate the sheet tab (right-click → Duplicate) and rename it for each goal: Emergency Fund, Travel, New Phone…

How much should I save monthly?

Use our free Savings Goal Calculator on this page's Calculators section to work out the monthly amount for any deadline.

Can I include interest or profit on savings?

Add it as a deposit row labelled 'Interest' — it counts toward your goal the same way.
