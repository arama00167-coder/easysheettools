About EasySheetTools

EasySheetTools exists for one reason: most people do not need expensive software to run a business, manage money or pass exams — they need one good spreadsheet and a clear explanation.

What we make

We publish free Excel templates, online calculators and step-by-step tutorials in three promises: every file must be genuinely useful, every formula must be checked before it goes live, and everything must work for a visitor in New York, London, Dubai, Toronto or Karachi equally well — any currency, any country.

Who is behind it

EasySheetTools is built by an Excel specialist in training — foundation-qualified and currently completing Advanced Excel — who spent years watching small business owners, students and freelancers struggle with the same five spreadsheets. This site is those spreadsheets, done properly, given away free.

Meet Sheetu

Our mascot Sheetu is the friendly grid character you will see around the site. Sheetu's job is simple: remind you that Excel is not scary. Sheetu makes Excel easy.

Our quality checks

  • Every template is recalculated before publishing — totals, schedules and balances must land exactly right.
  • Every file is tested in both Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
  • No macros, no hidden sheets, no locked cells you cannot edit.
  • Pages show a last-updated date, and tools are revised when readers report issues.
