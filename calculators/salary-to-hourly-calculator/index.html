Salary to Hourly Calculator

Comparing a salaried job with hourly contract work? Convert either direction with your real hours and working weeks, and see the full picture: hourly, weekly, monthly and annual pay side by side.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose whether you know the annual salary or the hourly rate.
  2. Enter the amount, your hours per week and weeks worked per year.
  3. Read every equivalent figure instantly.

The formula behind it

Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ (Hours per Week × Weeks per Year)

With the common 40 hours × 52 weeks, that is salary ÷ 2,080 — which is why a 60,000 salary works out to about 28.85 per hour before tax.

Worked example

A 60,000 annual salary at 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year = 28.85/hour, 1,153.85/week and 5,000/month.

Frequently asked questions

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax — it converts gross pay. Take-home pay depends on your country's tax and deductions.

I get two weeks of holiday. What weeks should I enter?

For comparing job offers, 52 shows the employer's true cost. For pricing your own freelance time, use the weeks you actually work — see the Hourly Rate Calculator Template.

How many hours are in a work year?

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks: 2,080 hours — the standard full-time figure in the US.
