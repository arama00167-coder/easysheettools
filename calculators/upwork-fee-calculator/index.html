Upwork Fee Calculator

Upwork's fee depends on how much you have billed that client over your lifetime: 20%, 10% or 5%. Enter the contract amount and your tier to see the fee, your real earnings, and what to charge to hit a target income.

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · ✓ Formula checked · Free, no sign-up

Your Upwork contract

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the amount the client will pay.
  2. Select your fee tier for this client (20%, 10% or 5%).
  3. See what lands in your account — and what to charge to earn a target amount after fees.

The formula behind it

You Receive = Contract Amount × (1 − Fee %)

Upwork charges the freelancer a service fee on every payment: 20% on the first $500 billed to a client, 10% on billings from $500.01 to $10,000, and 5% above $10,000 (lifetime, per client).

Worked example

A 1,000 contract with a new client (20% tier) pays you 800. With a long-term client in the 10% tier, the same contract pays 900.

Frequently asked questions

Which fee tier am I in?

It is per client, based on everything you have ever billed them. Brand-new client = 20%. Cross $500 lifetime with them and new earnings drop to 10%.

Does the client also pay a fee?

Yes, clients pay their own separate marketplace fee — it does not come out of your amount.

Can I track fees across all my jobs?

Yes — the free Freelancer Income Tracker applies the right tier per payment and totals your real income.
