The formula behind it

X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y

For a share: (part ÷ whole) × 100. For change: (new − old) ÷ old × 100 — positive means an increase, negative a decrease.

Worked example

20% of 150 is 30 (a typical tip or discount). Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase. Scoring 30 out of 120 on a test is 25%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount price?

Work out the % of the price with tool 1, then subtract it: 20% off 150 = 150 − 30 = 120.

Why is a 50% loss not undone by a 50% gain?

Percentages use different bases: 100 → 50 is −50%, but 50 → 100 is +100%. Tool 3 makes this visible.

Can I use this for exam marks?

Yes — tool 2 turns marks into a percentage; for letter grades use the Test Grade Calculator.
