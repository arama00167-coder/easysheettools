Weighted Grade Calculator

When your course grade is built from pieces — assignments 20%, midterm 30%, final 50% — a simple average lies to you. This calculator weights every piece properly and tells you exactly what score you need on the final exam.

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · ✓ Formula checked · Free, no sign-up

Enter your graded work

How to use this calculator

  1. Add a row for each graded item you have completed.
  2. Enter its weight (% of the total course grade) and your score %.
  3. Enter the final exam's weight and the overall grade you are aiming for.
  4. Read the exact score you need on the final.

The formula behind it

Points Earned = Σ (Score % × Weight %) ÷ 100

Each item contributes score × weight to your final 100 points. The score needed on the final is (Target − Points Earned) ÷ Final Weight — pure algebra, done for you.

Worked example

Assignments (20% weight, 88% score) earn 17.6 points and a midterm (30%, 82%) earns 24.6. With a 50%-weight final, you need (90 − 42.2) ÷ 0.5 = 95.6% for a 90 overall — tough but possible.

Frequently asked questions

What if the needed score is over 100%?

Then that target is mathematically out of reach with the remaining weight — aim for the next grade band and protect it instead.

My weights do not add up to 100. Is that OK?

The completed weights plus the final's weight should total 100. If your teacher uses different totals, scale the weights so they do.

Can teachers use this too?

Yes — enter a student's scores to show them exactly where they stand before the final.
